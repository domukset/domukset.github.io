13:00 - New Break in is discovered by residents. Club Room AND Sauna this time.


Aamulehti article
https://www.aamulehti.fi/tampere/art-2000012159369.html

Some interesting comments in the article:

Ten years ago, when I lived in a TOAS apartment, a drug user who was staying in the building broke into all the storage lockers and stole bicycles from the bike storage area. He was caught red-handed many times and had, among other things, taken items he’d pilfered from other people’s lockers and put them in his own. Numerous criminal reports were filed with the police and complaints were made to TOAS. The police did nothing, and TOAS stated that this kind of thing is just part of living in a TOAS apartment and you just have to put up with it. Nothing seems to have changed in the past ten years when it comes to living in a TOAS apartment.

No one is really interested in setting things up so that a break-in isn’t possible or would be as difficult as possible, because… The maintenance company will have extra billable work as a result of the incident. The cleaning company will have extra billable work. The locksmith company will have extra billable work. The property management company will have extra billable work. So why stop a good money-maker when, in the end, the tenant is the one paying for it all? It’s important, however, to empathize with the residents’ distress and at least appear to be doing something. “You’ve filed a police report, right? You have home insurance, don’t you? And these things happen all the time, don’t they?” These are important phrases that express sympathy for what’s happened, even though we know they don’t offer much comfort. You can get inside through the combination locks on the front doors with a little effort, etc. There are numerous ways to disable an electric lock on a front door so that it no longer works. You can break into the metal doors of storage units in a minute with a crowbar, since the doors and frames are made of sheet metal. If there’s enough clearance and the latch doesn’t have a deadbolt, you can get inside almost without leaving a trace. And this charade just goes on and on, and nobody really cares—everyone benefits, well, except for the victim of the break-in, but who really cares when these things just keep happening...?

TOAS could stop skimping on this issue and install surveillance cameras instead of relying on residents to keep an eye on things. Furthermore, the fact that the police don’t dare to go out and catch thieves in the act is pathetic—it’s as if they want to let the thieves get away. They certainly have enough courage for traffic enforcement, though—against taxpayers.

21.7.2026 TOAS CLOSES THE SAUNA +

The common room of the property was closed last week due to repeated vandalism and unauthorized people spending time in the common room. Due to recent events, we are extending the closures to also include the sauna and fireplace room. These facilities will remain closed until the situation calms down. We will inform the tenants when the facilities are ready to be reopened for use. A security guard has been and will continue to patrol the property more frequently. A locksmith has been assigned to inspect all exterior doors of the property and ensure that their mechanisms are working properly. There is some unrest in the Kaleva area, and several properties are facing the risk of break-ins. It is extremely important that, as a tenant, you submit a fault report if you notice that a door does not close properly. If you notice unauthorized people on the property, contact the police immediately.
Best regards
TOAS Property Management Department

Someone has also stuffed a wad of tissue paper into the hole to keep the lock from latching. I removed it but the lock didn't seem to spring back to the locked position as readily as before."


11:47 - "At this second two drunk none residents just broke to the terrace. Older guy and a very young woman. I asked do they live here and they said no, our friend does. I saw him already there and now it is confirmed they open the terrace door. Now they might took elevator to leave (another strike of door which left open) but they are not first time here. I am not going back there yet. The guy is having some clothes in the transparent plastic bag."

"I have seen them before at the lobby. Someone was letting them in the building so I thought their friend actually lived here despite looking super dodgy. This is the second time they try to open the club room. Lori and I just checked the emergency stairs and other places where we had people sleeping before, everything seems to be clean so no signs of squatting this time."

13:00 - "Dear TOAS, Once again we are reporting incidents in Uusi Domus related to the safety in the building. Outsiders keep accessing the building, going up to the roof top / terrace where they drink and trying to force entry into the club room once again (photo attached). We suspect they are trying to find a place to sleep since they are quite intoxicated. This was already reported 2 weeks ago, it is the same people as before. This happened today, Monday at midday so it is important to notice that it is not a late Saturday night event because some residents partied too much. These people are clearly outsiders. Sometimes they hangout/drink outside the building, where it used to be the restaurant's terrace (the restaurant is now closed). Also, another door to access the building was blocked with paper inside its lock space to prevent it from closing thus letting outsiders open it without a key (photo attached). Two weeks ago TOAS sent a notification to all the residents of Uusi Domus asking them not to let strangers in but our building has a gym in the basement, many people from nearby buildings have access to it so there is a constant flow of people into the building that we can not control nor inform about the safety issues we are facing. This adds another layer of complexity to the issue. We used to have a security company that would "patrol" the building at random times, mostly at night. But nobody has seen those "patrols" for a long time (1-2 years maybe). As explained in our previous email, closing the club room did not convince anyone here since we failed to understand how that relates to the security issues the building is experiencing. It was an ineffective and counterproductive measure, leaving the top floor area (club room / terrace) less frequented by tenants and more inviting for outsiders to use and abuse without anyone seeing them. The residents are very concerned."

21:30 - "They managed to break in the club room. They are sleeping there. They have an axe and drug cooking stuff, I will take it and call the police" - Police entered the club room at 22:28 but the squatters were not present, police took the axe and the chemistry lab containers


Dear TOAS, the situation at Uusi Domus keeps escalating and it's getting way out of hand. TOAS' silence is not helping anyone. Thanks to the Club Room being closed and not accessed by anybody in the building, it became the perfect hideout for drug addicts to squat it and settle down there. They failed to break in through the door TWICE but they managed to use the broken windows that we reported to TOAS in multiple occasions but were never fixed properly. We found hard drugs paraphernalia, an axe and other blades (handed over to the police, photos attached). Two police vans came, but the squatters/addicts left the club room before police arrived. They did not see the police so they will come back at some point and they will keep trying to gain access to the building. In our previous email we inquired about the private security company that used to visit the building at night time. Some tenants felt quite scared and disappointed today, they were organizing a group visit to the TOAS office to demand action in person. We explained to them that it is better to use written forms of communication so the messages can be forwarded to the right people instead of expressing their frustration to the first person they will meet in front of a desk. We totally understand that this is a complex issue, the whole society is facing the side effects of the economic situation that the country is living in at the moment, but please take safety reports more seriously in the future. The tenants have expressed their disappointment with TOAS recent responses, sending emails saying "please don't open the door to strangers" and closing the club room was far from ideal. Please see photos attached, it got quite hectic here.

13.7.2026 TOAS CLOSING OF THE COMMON ROOM +

After finding out there was a party on 29.6 that ended with the police present because party goers climbed on the roof top of the building, TOAS decides to close the Club Room to all residents citing vandalism in the premises, which was NOT caused by the party. It is very possible that the squatters were already using the room at night time.

"Unfortunately, we fail to understand how closing the club room is related to the security issues we have been having in the building recently. The security issues are related to building access through the main doors and the outdated fingerprint system, not the club room. Residents are feeling punished for no reason with TOAS unilateral decision, they are asking what can be done to solve it as soon as possible. What about setting a one4all system in the Uusi Domus? It seems to be the best solution."

13.7.2026 First reports of strangers around the building +

"I noticed two totally unfamiliar people at the terrace during the weekend, being there the whole day. Looks like an older couple. Not going to use metaphor to describe them but definitely not students. I was really surprised finding them at the sauna doors every time I go for a cig often. I had the idea to inform here at chat but I was hesitating to do something wrong, maybe they are someone’s parents, I thought. So maybe openned terrace doors were part of their squatting the Domus."

22.5.2026 Storage Burglary +

In the past few days someone entered the storage area in Uusi Domus and broke into several lockers by force. After a quick inspection we saw that affected lockers include at least 10 units, maybe more. Please check your belongings and report any theft to TOAS and the police. TOAS sent a general email for tenants to check their storages for possible stolen items.
