20.7.2026 More signs of people entering the building +

8:00 - "This morning the lever that actuates the lock on the basement door leading to the bike storage and laundry room etc. was in the down (open) position. Someone has also stuffed a wad of tissue paper into the hole to keep the lock from latching. I removed it but the lock didn't seem to spring back to the locked position as readily as before."

11:47 - "At this second two drunk none residents just broke to the terrace. Older guy and a very young woman. I asked do they live here and they said no, our friend does. I saw him already there and now it is confirmed they open the terrace door. Now they might took elevator to leave ( another strike of door which left open) but they are not first time here. I am not going back there yet. The guy is having some clothes in the transparent plastic bag."

"I have seen them before at the lobby. Someone was letting them in the building so I thought their friend actually lived here despite looking super dodgy"

13.7.2026 TOAS CLOSING OF THE COMMON ROOM +

After finding out there was a party on 29.6 that ended with the police present because party goers climbed on the roof top of the building, TOAS decides to close the Club Room to all residents citing vandalism in the premises, which was NOT caused by the party. It is very possible that the squatters were already using the room at night time.

"Unfortunately, we fail to understand how closing the club room is related to the security issues we have been having in the building recently. The security issues are related to building access through the main doors and the outdated fingerprint system, not the club room. Residents are feeling punished for no reason with TOAS unilateral decision, they are asking what can be done to solve it as soon as possible. What about setting a one4all system in the Uusi Domus? It seems to be the best solution."

13.7.2026 First reports of strangers around the building +

"I noticed two totally unfamiliar people at the terrace during the weekend, being there the whole day. Looks like an older couple. Not going to use metaphor to describe them but definitely not students. I was really surprised finding them at the sauna doors every time I go for a cig often. I had the idea to inform here at chat but I was hesitating to do something wrong, maybe they are someone’s parents, I thought. So maybe openned terrace doors were part of their squatting the Domus."

22.5.2026 Storage Burglary +

In the past few days someone entered the storage area in Uusi Domus and broke into several lockers by force. After a quick inspection we saw that affected lockers include at least 10 units, maybe more. Please check your belongings and report any theft to TOAS and the police. TOAS sent a general email for tenants to check their storages for possible stolen items.
