13.7.2026 TOAS CLOSING OF THE COMMON ROOM +

After finding out there was a party on 29.6 that ended with the police present because party goers climbed on the roof top of the building, TOAS decides to close the Club Room to all residents citing vandalism in the premises, which was NOT caused by the party. It is very possible that the squatters were already using the room at night time.

"Unfortunately, we fail to understand how closing the club room is related to the security issues we have been having in the building recently. The security issues are related to building access through the main doors and the outdated fingerprint system, not the club room. Residents are feeling punished for no reason with TOAS unilateral decision, they are asking what can be done to solve it as soon as possible. What about setting a one4all system in the Uusi Domus? It seems to be the best solution."

22.5.2026 Storage Burglary +

In the past few days someone entered the storage area in Uusi Domus and broke into several lockers by force. After a quick inspection we saw that affected lockers include at least 10 units, maybe more. Please check your belongings and report any theft to TOAS and the police. TOAS sent a general email for tenants to check their storages for possible stolen items.
